# Surface brief: the story (route `/`) and the shell around the three views

Mode: Experience, handing over to Operate views. Audience: a reviewer at a laptop in a lit room, the builder beside
them. Job: understand in a few minutes what the instrument watches and how to read an alert, then enter the live map.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the day's landslide chart is plotted in front of you. Scrolling does the plotting: contour lines, then relief,
then rain, then warnings, and the last chapter is the live map itself. It refuses the dark mission-control dashboard
and the hero-plus-cards landing page.

OWN-WORLD: the daily synoptic weather chart and its bulletin. Pale chart paper, indigo ink, sepia contour lines, a
blue plotting accent, and the weather service's own warning colours (yellow, orange, red) used only for levels.
Annotation slips pasted on the chart carry the copy; a margin scale carries position. Monoline routed lettering for
names and headings, a plain legible text face, a tabular mono for measurements.

STORY: the ground decides where; the rain decides when; the levels say how sure; today's chart is the result; the
prototype says what it cannot do.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the whole subcontinent as bare contour lines being drawn on chart paper, the name set large across the
sea at lower left, the bulletin line (run date, live or replay, prototype notice) under it, one action: read the chart.

FORM: grounded candidate 3 of 7 (the daily weather chart); seed f1475884. Kept from the dealt hand: ink that spreads
like dye for rain (suminagashi), nothing hidden about how it is made (exposed binding), a position rail (timetable).

FINISH: unreviewed and unshipped until the inspection rounds and the detector pass have run.
